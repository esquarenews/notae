Notae keeps the record direct, then lets emphasis move naturally through the sentence without changing its texture. Upright and italic maintain nearly identical lowercase width averages—517 and 516.8 units—so lines stay settled when a phrase changes style.

Each WOFF2 contains every weight from 100–900 for one style. Both expose the same 470 codepoints; the italic retains the app-facing kerning, mark, localization, contextual and numeral features while omitting unrelated source alternates.

Family specification

One calm system, from Thin upright to Black italic.

The production pair is self-hostable, locally installable and metric-aligned for UI controls, documents, dense metadata and editorial emphasis. The build remains reproducible from its two pinned OFL sources.
